## Final Outcome

This project successfully built a machine learning pipeline to predict customer churn with strong accuracy using Random Forest as the best-performing model. 
Based on the analysis, the key drivers of churn are:

- Contract type – Customers on month-to-month contracts are significantly more likely to churn compared to those on longer-term plans.
- Tenure – Newer customers (shorter tenure) show a higher churn rate, indicating early-stage retention is critical.
- Monthly charges – Higher billing amounts correlate with increased churn probability.
- Support services – Customers without tech support or security add-ons are more likely to leave.

Business Recommendation: To reduce churn, the company should focus on incentivizing long-term contracts, improving onboarding for new customers, 
and promoting tech support/security bundles. Especially for high-paying customers on flexible plans.
